## Description

A simple bar handle for cabinets and drawers. It is 200 mm long with 160 mm spacing between the holes, and has finger
grooves on both sides. The holes are made for 8 mm long M4 heat-set inserts.

I printed it using the following configuration: Prusa i3 MK3S, PLA, 15% infill, 0.15 mm layer height, brim enabled, and
4 perimeters around the inserts.
